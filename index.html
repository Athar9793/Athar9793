Nov 2026

5 Common MEP Coordination Clashes and How to Avoid Them

Most site rework comes from a handful of repeat clashes between ducts, cable trays and drainage.

Read more

Typical clashes: main ducts vs. beams, cable trays vs. sprinkler mains, gravity drainage vs. ductwork, plant access vs. services, and ceiling voids that are simply too shallow. Fix them early with a coordinated BIM model, agreed service zones per discipline, and clash reviews before shop drawings are approved.

Oct 2026

How to Build an Accurate MEP Cost Estimate

A reliable MEP estimate depends on clean quantities, current rates and honest allowances.

Read more

Start from a measured take-off by system, not by drawing. Price materials from recent supplier quotes, build labour from realistic productivity, and add separate lines for testing and commissioning, preliminaries and risk. Review the BOQ against the specification so nothing specified is left unpriced.

Sep 2026

Planned Preventive Maintenance: Where FM Savings Really Come From

A good PPM programme cuts breakdowns, extends asset life and lowers energy bills.

Read more

Build a complete asset register first, then set maintenance frequencies by criticality and manufacturer guidance. Track completion rates and repeat faults, and use the data to adjust the plan. Chillers, AHUs and pumps usually offer the biggest energy and reliability gains.
