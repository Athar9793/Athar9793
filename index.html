About Us

Azmi MEP Consultants is an engineering consultancy based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. We bring together MEP engineering, commercial and project controls expertise under one roof, so clients get technical quality and cost and schedule certainty from the same team.

Our team of MEP engineers has worked on commercial, residential, education, entertainment, district cooling and renewable energy projects across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and India.

Why clients work with us

  • One team for engineering, cost and planning
  • Experience across KSA, UAE and India
  • Work to Saudi Building Code and international standards
  • Clear reporting and responsive communication

FAQ

What types of projects do you work on?

Commercial, residential, education, entertainment, district cooling and renewable energy projects, covering HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire protection systems.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Both. Design review, estimation and planning can be done remotely; supervision and commissioning are on-site.

How do you price your services?

Hourly, fixed-fee or monthly retainer, depending on scope. Request a quotation through the contact form or WhatsApp.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the project scope. We confirm the start date with our quotation.
