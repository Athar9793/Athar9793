Facilities Management

Well-run building services cost less and last longer. We help owners set up and improve planned preventive maintenance, asset registers and operating procedures for MEP systems.

What we deliver

  • Planned preventive maintenance (PPM) programmes
  • Asset registers
  • Building condition assessments
  • O&M optimisation
  • Maintenance contract and SLA review
  • Energy efficiency reviews

Our approach

01

Survey

Survey assets and assess their condition.

02

Plan

Set PPM frequencies by criticality and manufacturer guidance.

03

Implement

Prepare work orders, checklists and CAFM-ready data.

04

Improve

Track KPIs, analyse repeat faults and review energy use.

Who it's for

  • Owners of schools, offices and commercial buildings
  • FM contractors improving service delivery
  • Organisations taking over new facilities

Frequently asked questions

Can you help set up a CAFM system?

We prepare asset data, PPM schedules and checklists in a format ready to load into your CAFM or CMMS system.

Do you carry out the maintenance work?

We provide consultancy and supervision. The maintenance work itself is carried out by your FM contractor.
